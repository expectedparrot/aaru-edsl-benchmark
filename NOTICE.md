# Data provenance and third-party material

The question wording, audience descriptions, reference shares, source metadata,
and Aaru predictions originate in Aaru's public benchmark export:

- [Research publication](https://aaru.com/publications/population-simulation-at-the-replication-floor-research)
- [Question export](https://aaru.com/data/population-simulation/questions.json)

The archived export was retrieved September 24, 2026. Each question retains its
original survey-source link. `data/provenance.json` records the original byte
hash and export transformations. The snapshot preserves the original data,
including its caveats and metadata; it is not an independently corrected survey
database.

The MIT license applies to original code in this repository, not to third-party
survey materials, Aaru's predictions, names, or marks. No blanket redistribution
license for the source benchmark was identified on the research page. This
repository does not purport to relicense those materials or grant rights to
their further use. Attribution is not a substitute for permission where needed.

The Astra, Fable, and Gemini answer texts were generated in this independent
experiment through EDSL / Expected Parrot. Response records retain answer text,
token usage, price metadata, and prompts but omit provider/account identifiers
and encrypted or summarized reasoning. EP job UUIDs in the cost audit identify
private accounting records; no credential is included. Archive hashes verify
integrity, not independent provenance.

The authors of the source surveys and Aaru have not endorsed this analysis.
